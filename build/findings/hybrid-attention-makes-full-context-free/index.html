FINDING / 2026-09-06

Hybrid attention made full 262K context nearly free on a 12 GB card

Qwen3.8-Flash-Next runs its full native 262144-token context on the RTX 3060 Unraid box because only 12 of 48 layers carry a KV cache; the master rebuild gained speed even while raising context from 128K to 262K.

The qwen4_exp architecture layers 12 QSA sparse-attention layers among 36 Gated DeltaNet layers. The GDN layers compress history into a fixed-size recurrent state and hold no cache; the 12 QSA layers use only 2 KV heads at 256 head dimension. At 262144 tokens the entire KV cache at q8_0 is about 3.4 GB, and the GDN state is negligible. A Qwen3.5-class dense-attention model of similar size would need tens of gigabytes for the same context.

The Unraid server was moved from a 131072-token context on an August 29 build to the full 262144-token context on September 6 master, which carried seven qwen4exp fixes including a GDN normalization correctness fix merged the same day, CUDA sparse-attention kernels, and a graph-split reduction. Decode measured 14.3 and 15.1 tok/s across 400-token runs, slightly faster than the 13.3 tok/s the older build recorded at 128K.

An ncmoe sweep mapped the expert-offload frontier: moving four to six of the 48 expert layers onto the GPU lifts short-context decode from 13.6 to about 18.9 tok/s, but those configurations exceed the 12 GB card once the full-context KV and compute buffers are allocated. Peak short-context throughput and full long-context capacity are mutually exclusive on this hardware; the serving configuration keeps all expert layers on CPU to hold the full 262144-token slot.

The model is wired into OpenCode as qwen38-unraid/qwen38-flash with the 262144-token limit declared, replacing the role the removed K2-Horizon build played.
