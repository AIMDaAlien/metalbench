FINDING / 2026-09-02

A companion model needs more than a score

The strongest compact text score did not make a replacement for a screen-aware companion, and voice selection stayed a listening decision.

Three compact candidates ran the same ten deterministic field tasks. Nanbeige recorded 8/10, current Qwen3.5 VLM 6/10, and Spark-X2.5 4/10. Those scores describe instruction, format, code, planning, extraction, and safety behavior; they are not a universal intelligence ranking.

A separate twelve-probe transcript cohort tested truthful awareness, unknown-memory honesty, correction, quiet company, conversational register, role trade-offs, reference resolution, source honesty, English-only output, kind accountability, short-thread retrieval, and counterfactual reasoning. No candidate earned a direct replacement promotion. Nanbeige and Spark are text-only; current Qwen remains the visual fast lane but needs behavioral regression fixes.

Speech used a third, end-to-end method: English reply, private Japanese translation, synthesis, then first playable external-output audio. Irodori v4.1 with the retained JA-B identity was selected by listening for emotional fit. Its warmed full chain measured 1.852 seconds. That is a product casting decision, not a claim of a universal voice winner.
